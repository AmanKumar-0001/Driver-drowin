## 2. Subject-Wise Partitioning Mandate
To avoid spatial correlation and data leakage across video frames of the same individual, all dataset partitioning is conducted strictly on a **per-subject basis**:
* **Train:** $70\%$ of distinct subjects.
* **Validation:** $15\%$ of distinct subjects.
* **Test:** $15\%$ of distinct subjects.

Partition assignments are generated by `scripts/prepare_data.py` and locked in `data/splits.json` to guarantee exact scientific reproducibility.

---

## 3. Data Storage & Privacy Policy
* Raw and processed image datasets are strictly git-ignored via `.gitignore` (`data/`).
* External datasets must not be committed to the code repository.
* Download scripts or external links must be utilized by researchers.
